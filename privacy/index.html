Privacy Policy

Effective 21 August 2026 · Last updated 21 August 2026

Jannah is a prayer-times and worship app. This page is the policy for the Android app published as com.allahpolice.app.

We collect as little as the app needs to work, keep most of it on your phone, and do not sell it.

What we collect

Phone number

During first-run you give a mobile number. We send a one-time SMS code to confirm it is yours. That number is stored on the device and with Google Firebase Authentication, which delivers the SMS and holds the account. We do not use the number for marketing.

Location

Prayer times need a place. If you allow location, the app reads an approximate position once, turns it into a city name, and stores coordinates on the device. You can refuse and pick a city yourself. Location is not sent to our servers. On Android, reverse-geocoding may use Google Play services on the device.

Kept only on your phone

  • Language, gender, hometown and prayer-calculation choices.
  • Notification preferences and whether menstrual mode is on.
  • Daily goals, bookmarks and onboarding progress.

These live in the app’s local storage (SharedPreferences). They are not uploaded to us.

What we do not collect

We do not run analytics, crash reporters or advertising SDKs in this app. We do not read your contacts, photos or SMS inbox. The gallery wallpapers are public images we host; the app does not upload your pictures.

Who sees it

Google Firebase

Phone verification is provided by Google. Firebase receives the number in order to send the SMS and to create the account. Their handling of that data is described in Google’s Firebase privacy policy.

Cloudflare

Gallery photographs are served from a Cloudflare R2 bucket. That request is for a public image file. It does not include your phone number or location.

On-device services

Prayer times are calculated on the device (Adhan). Qibla uses the compass. Athan alerts are local notifications scheduled on the phone. None of those leave the device as a report to us.

Why we hold it

  • To create and secure your account (phone number, SMS code).
  • To compute prayer times and Qibla for the place you chose.
  • To remember settings, goals and bookmarks between launches.
  • To play the athan at the times you asked for.

Legal bases, where a law asks for one: the phone number is processed to provide the account you requested; location is processed with your permission, or from a city you typed yourself.

How long, and how to delete

Local settings stay on the phone until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes everything stored on that device.

The Firebase account (your verified number) stays until you ask us to delete it. Email privacy@allahpolice.app from a reachable address, name the number on the account, and we will delete it. We aim to do that within 30 days.

You can also change or remove the number inside Settings after sign-in. Revoking location permission stops further reads; the last saved city remains until you change it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use after that date is acceptance of the new text.
